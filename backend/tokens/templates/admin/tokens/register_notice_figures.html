Notice figures

Prepare notice figures only on the company's written instruction: the company decides which notices of share issues and of changes to its members and share structure to lodge, and lodges them. The CSV lists every issue, transfer and correction entry dated on or after the first day of the period, up to the register's latest entry, one row for each member it changed, with the shares as a signed change. It then gives the class's issued supply, the number of members holding shares and the total amount paid, and the name, residential address, shares held and amount paid of each member those entries changed. Amount paid is shown only where the register establishes it exactly; otherwise it says "not recorded". Days are counted in Sydney's calendar. Ledova records the period, the instruction and the file's SHA-256, not the file.
