Certificate for a register entry

Prepare certificates only on the company's written instruction. Enter the number of an issue or transfer entry in this share class's register; an entry a correction has reversed has no certificate. The PDF has a page for the member the entry moved shares to, and for a transfer a balance certificate for the transferor if they still hold shares. Pages are numbered from the entry, such as 12-1 and 12-2, and show the member's name and residential address, the shares, the holding after the entry, and the entry's number and hash. A member who is not identified by a name and residential address, or whose wallets resolve to different people, stops the whole certificate. The file is unsigned: give it to the company to execute. Ledova records the entry, the instruction and the file's SHA-256, not the file.
